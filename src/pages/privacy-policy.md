This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the Platform collects, uses, maintains and discloses information of its users. By accessing and using the Platform, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of information described in this Policy.

## 1. Personal Information

**Personal Information** means information which identifies you — first and last name, email address, age, gender, location, photograph and/or phone number provided at the time of registration.

**Sensitive Personal Information** includes passwords, financial data, health data, official identifiers (Aadhaar, passport, etc.), and other data categorised as sensitive under applicable Data Protection Laws.

## 2. Information We Collect

- We do not ask for Personal Information unless we truly need it.
- We do not share your Personal Information with anyone except to comply with applicable laws.
- We do not store Personal Information on servers unless required for ongoing operations.
- **Personal Identifiable Information:** Name and email address to enable access to the Platform.
- **Non-Personal Information:** Browser name, language, referring site, date and time of requests, operating system.
- **Cookies:** Used to enhance user experience. You may choose to disable cookies in your browser.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide access to our Platform and services.
- To improve our Platform and maintain safety.
- To communicate with you or market our services/products.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise exploit your personal information to others.

## 4. Your Rights

- **Right to Confirmation and Access** — Access your Personal Information held by us.
- **Right to Correction** — Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Right to be Forgotten** — Restrict continuing disclosure of your information.
- **Right to Erasure** — Request deletion of your Personal Information from our Platform.

## 5. Protection of Your Information

We take all measures reasonably necessary to protect against unauthorized access, use, alteration or destruction of Personal Information. Disclosure is limited to employees and contractors bound by confidentiality, and where required by law.

## 6. Third Party Websites

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Browsing any linked website is subject to that site's own terms and policies.

## 7. Data Storage

Your information is stored and processed on Amazon Web Service (AWS) servers in India. We will retain your information for as long as required for the purposes stated in this Policy.

## 8. Modifications

We may modify this Policy from time to time. We encourage you to check our Platform frequently for recent changes.

## 9. Grievances

For questions, concerns or grievances about this Policy, please write to us at **support@natarajmalavade.in**
